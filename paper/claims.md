# Claims register (Helix technical report)

**Rule: no row, no claim.** Every claim in `report.en.md`, `report.fr.md` and the article has a row here. A claim without evidence is removed from the report or reworded until the evidence supports it. Before any release, every number is checked against the raw results, and the English and French versions are checked against each other.

Evidence names a journal id (`../journal/`) and the file holding the raw value. Section numbers refer to `report.en.md`.
