3. The suites overwrote tracked shots of Phases 6 and 7 (`docs/reviews/phase-6-shots`, `phase-7-accessibility-audit`); restored from git, not committed.

## Limits / confounders

U-11 is fixed in part only: the panel after an answer keeps its sections. Bytes are measured for the nine changes together, not per change. One machine, Chrome only; Safari and Firefox not rerun (the Phase 12 build of record reruns the whole suite). The welcome's `chosen` mark relies on `history.replaceState`; a browser that blocks it would keep the mark in the address until the next gene choice.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis holds within what was tested: nine findings fixed for 671 bytes, no regression in the covering suites, and rules version 8 changes only the wording of a refusal, with identical scores on both frozen sets.

## Decision

**Continue** to the Phase 12 build of record (step 4 of the plan). For the author: P12-U-01 (limitation, or include the chapter's citations among the panel's cards), and whether U-11 needs more than removing the repeated questions.
