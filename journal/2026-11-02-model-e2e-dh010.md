## Methods

The suites of HX-P6-2026-11-02-model-e2e-dh008, unchanged, plus a new Phase 6 scenario `dh010-routing` whose expectations were committed with the build (helix-app b4afe5e) before its first run:

1. Fresh page, journey off (DNA World): "Take me to the sickle-cell change in beta-globin."; expect an executed plan in Protein World only, VAL B6, D6, F6 and H6 selected, no bridge offered.
2. Fresh page, navigate to Protein World, then "Which codon of hemoglobin carries the sickle-cell change?": DNA World only; the bridge is offered first.
3. Welcome open, type HBB in the gene field and explore: the welcome closes, the journey starts on the transcript chapter in DNA World, no structure is loaded, and no question is asked of any planner.

Cases 1 and 2 run once with the rule-based planner and once with the model; case 3 once (no model involved). The wordings differ from the benchmark and held-out items.

## Failures

None in the runs. As on the benchmark (HX-P7-2026-11-02-routing-dh010, items RM02 to RM04 and RM08), the model itself answered the beta-globin question in DNA World despite prompt v5; the world-cue check caught it and the rule-based plan answered, so the visitor waited for the model and then got the rule-based answer (23.6 s in total). Reported as measured, n = 1.

## Limits / confounders

- One machine, headless Chrome, few runs; n = 1 for the D-H010 cases.
- The D-H010 questions are close in kind to the D-H010 held-out items; they check the app path, not new wording.

## Interpretation

Supported: on the D-H010 build every model-backed and rule-based scenario passes; a molecule-name question asked in DNA World ends on the residue in Protein World, a DNA cue still beats a molecule name, and the welcome's gene choice is unchanged. The executed answer is right, but for the molecule-name question it comes from the rule-based fallback after a rejected model plan, which costs the model's planning time. Model planning times are within 0.5 s of the D-H008 build on the shared scenarios.
