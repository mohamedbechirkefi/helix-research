## Method

No suite was rerun. The review page (helix-app `docs/reviews/phase-12.md`) assembles the results of the build of record, S30 and S31, the documentation, the recording, the report and the article. It judges the plan's section 1 criterion by criterion and gives the state of each section 5 item.

For the text count, the two S21 inventories were compared after one normalisation applied to both:
- reference digits glued to a sentence end ("...A61.1,2,3", "...status.)2") removed;
- run timestamps replaced by a placeholder.

The normalisation was chosen after reading the raw differences.

## Raw results

| Measure | Gate (p11gate2) | Build of record (p12bor) |
|---|---|---|
| Distinct texts | 9,221 | 9,126 |
| After normalising | 8,944 (277 merged) | 8,958 (168 merged) |
| Only in this build after normalising | 14 | 28 |

The 42 remaining texts (`docs/reviews/phase-12-gate/text-count-diff.out`) are:
- the reference links;
- "On this page";
- Pathway World in the welcome kicker;
- "Play";
- the Mol* label without its repetition;
- the rules version 8 pathway refusal;
- the supported-questions line;
- the dynamic page's chapter state and exon labels, which depend on the live run;
- the research commit named on the dynamic page.

## Findings

- **The text count.** The drop is in the counting. Since P12.1.2 (U-10), reference numbers are links that the inventory reads as their own units ("Reference 1"). Sentences that differed only by their glued reference digits therefore count once. Every text of both builds maps to its source.
- **Gate state.** Every judged check passes. Every criterion holds as the review states it. Four items are for the author:
  - the publication itself (question 101);
  - the desktop tour items of D-H047 (1), not in the agreed fixes, proposed as stated;
  - P12-U-01, proposed as stated;
  - the author's two own checks before any release: the wordmark clearance and the ClinVar and RefSeq page visit.
- `docs/known-limitations.md`: the CSP row and the desktop first-frame rows were brought to the gate state.

## Uncertainty

The normalisation was chosen after seeing the differences. The dynamic page's content depends on the live retrieval of each run.

## Interpretation

Hypothesis (2) holds: no text of the gate is lost except through intended changes (C201). Hypothesis (1) holds as stated in the review. The verdict is the author's.
