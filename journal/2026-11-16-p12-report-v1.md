## Method

1. **Text.** The report and the article went from draft 0.9 and draft 1 to version 1.0 in both languages:
   - header and status lines;
   - section 9.10, "Publication (after the gate)": the verdict, the author's two checks, the public host and its results, the push incident, what stays open;
   - the limits on the CSP and on the author's checks;
   - the annex row of Phase 12;
   - the article's section 7.5 and its Availability section.
2. **Claims.** Two rows were added: C202 (the public host, from HX-P12-2026-11-15-public and HX-P12-2026-11-16-public-a11y) and C203 (the author's checks, from HX-P12-2026-11-15-ncbi-pages and the audit's P11-LIC-07 and P11-LIC-08).
3. **Figures.** Eight in the report, four of them in the article, each with the same caption in both languages:
   - the four architecture diagrams, copied from `docs/showcase/diagrams/`;
   - four frames of the public demo recording.
4. **Build.** The PDFs are built by a new script adapted from the author's Hive report build: pandoc to Typst, title page, outline, list of figures. Every page was read on contact sheets at 60 ppi.

## Findings

- **The first build ran lists into their paragraphs.** Lists written right after a sentence, with no blank line, came out as inline dashes. GitHub renders them, pandoc's default does not. The build now reads them with `lists_without_preceding_blankline`.
- **The cover page title was hyphenated.** It is now set without justification or hyphenation.
- **The French sources use the English number notation, as the parity check requires.** The French copies now state this in their header table, the one row more than the English.
- **Frame extraction.** The first extraction hung on a video frame callback in headless Chrome. A plain `seeked` wait with a timeout works.

## Uncertainty

- The PDFs depend on macOS system fonts.
- Frame times are those of the recording's shot list. Each frame was looked at, but not compared pixel by pixel with the app.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis holds. Version 1.0 of the report and the article is released as `v1.0-report`, on the author's instruction.
