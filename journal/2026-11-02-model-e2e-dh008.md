## Methods

The suites of HX-P6-2026-11-02-model-e2e-oneworld and HX-P6-2026-11-02-start-world-dh007, unchanged, plus a new Phase 6 scenario `dh008-routing` whose expectations were committed with the build (d9d8ded) before its first run:

1. Fresh page, journey off, welcome closed (DNA World): ask "Show me the sickle-cell mutation and keep it simple."; expect an executed plan whose actions show only DNA World (no structure loaded, stage on DNA), the comparison GAG to GTG when the plan compares; the first next-branch chip is "See the sickle-cell mutation on the protein." with source `bridge`; clicking it executes and selects VAL B6, D6, F6 and H6 in Protein World.
2. Fresh page: "Show me the sickle-cell mutation on the protein." asked in DNA World: Protein World only, the four residues selected, no bridge offered.
3. Fresh page, navigate to Protein World (not an answer), then "Show me the codon of the sickle-cell mutation.": DNA World only; the bridge is then offered first.
