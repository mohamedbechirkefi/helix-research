## Interpretation

All four answers are built. The duplicate zod came from the research packages being linked by path, not from two releases; one line removes it and gives the bytes budget about 25.8 kB of room. The question limit closes the one open security finding about input. The domain refusal no longer says something about UniProt that UniProt contradicts, and the rules' scores show the change is wording only. The frame budget of 16.8 ms has no margin on this machine today: the pre-pass build measures 17.0 ms on the same check.
