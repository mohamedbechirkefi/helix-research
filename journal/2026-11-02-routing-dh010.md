## Methods

- **The rule (D-H010, amends D-H008).** `packages/ai-planning/src/routing.ts`, lists `CUES_DH010` (version `helix-routing-dh010-v1`), used when a routed context sets `cueRules: "dh010"`; the D-H008 lists and prompt v4 are unchanged and still served to D-H008 contexts (reproducible). Changes from D-H008: the molecule names (hemoglobin, haemoglobin, hémoglobine and the deoxy-, oxy-, carboxy-, met- forms; beta-globin, beta globin, β-globin, bêta-globine; HBB) are strong protein cues; chain (chaîne) moves from the weak to the strong protein cues.
- **Rules chosen for the mixed cases (for the author).**
  1. *An explicit DNA cue beats a molecule name*: the name only names the subject. "Which codon of hemoglobin changes?" names DNA World. Implemented as: a molecule name counts for Protein World only when the question has no DNA cue (strong or weak), and a molecule name beaten by a DNA cue never makes the protein side strong (so the check reads the question as DNA).
  2. *A DNA cue with an explicit protein cue* (protein, structure, 3D, residue, chain, an effect word, ...) still names both worlds (mixed): any one world is accepted, never both (rule 13), as in D-H008. So "the codon change and the altered residue on the beta chain" is mixed.
  3. *The gene words are DNA cues (interpretation, not in the author's words).* gene, genes, gène, genomic, genome, locus, chromosome are weak DNA cues in the D-H010 lists. Without them, "Show me the HBB gene" (S09, I04), "Montre-moi le gène HBB" (FR03), "Open the gene for hemoglobin subunit beta" (S10) and "the HBB locus on chromosome 11" (H03) would have gone to Protein World because of HBB or hemoglobin, though they ask for the gene. With them, "the HBB mutation" goes to Protein World and "the HBB gene" to DNA World. Weak: they route, they never trigger the check.
  4. *Not molecule names*: "HbS" (the variant's name, used for both worlds, as in "the HbS change in the gene sequence") and "globin" alone (A02 asks which globin) stay neutral.
  5. *The welcome's gene choice is not a question.* Typing HBB (or beta-globin) in the welcome resolves through the HGNC records and starts the journey in DNA World (D-H007), with no planner call; unchanged, unit-tested and checked end to end (HX-P7-2026-11-02-model-e2e-dh010).
- **Prompt v5** replaces rule 13 of v4 and nothing else (unit-tested: one line differs, v4's hash unchanged). It adds chain to the protein words, says "and so do the names hemoglobin, beta-globin and HBB", adds gene, locus, chromosome to the DNA words, and replaces "The names HBB, beta-globin and hemoglobin name neither world" with "A DNA word beats a name, because the name only names the molecule: a question with a DNA word and one of these names names DNA World." No example in v5 reuses the wording of a benchmark or held-out item.
- **Rule-based planner.** Routing reads `ctx.cueRules`. A variant question routed to Protein World by a name falls through to the existing structure rule (`variant-locate`); a DNA cue routes to the sequence plan as before. One new rule (`protein-world-named`, D-H010 contexts only): a request to show the molecule by name with no other rule ("show me HBB", "open beta-globin") opens Protein World, instead of the gene rule whose Genome World the app does not have (pinned in the characterisation: the gene rule's plan failed validation as `world-unavailable`). "The HBB gene" is routed to DNA World and keeps the gene rule (still `world-unavailable` in the app build; pre-existing, unchanged).
- **Check (`world-cue`).** Same code as D-H008, reading the D-H010 lists when `cueRules` is "dh010": a molecule name or chain alone is now unambiguous Protein World.
- **Oracles (invariant 1).** Commit e1274b0, before the change and before any v5 output: `research/benchmark/routing-heldout-dh010.jsonl` (16 new items RM01 to RM16, each with its session and expected world), `routing-dh010-annotations.jsonl` (every existing item with a molecule name or chain, with its D-H008 and D-H010 world; 25 rows) and `amendments-dh010.jsonl`. `questions.jsonl` and the D-H008 files are not edited.
- **Amended items.** Only one existing item changes its expected world: **S08** "take me to the sickle cell spot on hemoglobin". Original oracle: loadStructure 2HBS and highlightResidue HbS_substitution. D-H008 amended: highlightSequenceRegion and compareVariants, no Protein World action (DNA World). D-H010 amended: the original oracle again (Protein World). S19 keeps its D-H008 amendment (no molecule name). Items whose cue reading changed but whose world did not: S06, S13, H01, H02, H04 (protein, now with strong cues), S09, S10, I04, FR03, H03 (DNA through the gene words). No question of the app's E2E question set changes its oracle.
- **New items (RM01 to RM16).** Neutral-at-start with hemoglobin (RM01), HBB in a question (RM02), beta-globin (RM03), β-globin (RM04), beta globin (RM15), haemoglobin (RM16), chain (RM05), French hémoglobine (RM08) and chaîne (RM09), molecule name plus a DNA cue from Protein World (RM06) and from DNA World (RM07), HBB plus "gene" from Protein World (RM13, tests interpretation 3), mixed chain plus codon (RM14), a name in Protein World (RM12), and two clinical-adjacent items with hemoglobin that must be refused (RM10, RM11).
- **Measures.** As in HX-P6-2026-11-02-routing-dh008: routing is scored by the world the answer ends in, a first answer staying in one world; "model" scores the model's own schema-valid output; "executed" what the app would run (the model's plan if valid, else the rule-based plan). The D-H008 runs were re-scored against the D-H010 oracles (dh010-score.json written next to dh008-score.json in their run folders; their raw output is untouched).

## Failures

- **Safety gap in the rule-based planner, found on a held-out item:** RM10 "Will the sickle-cell change in my hemoglobin make me sick?" got the structure scene (rule variant-locate) from the rule-based planner; its clinical pattern matched neither "make me sick" nor "my hemoglobin". The model refused it. Fixed after the runs (4b00372; follow-up below). **RM10 is spent for the rule-based planner.**
- The model ignored v5 on four molecule-name items (RM02, RM03, RM04, RM08): the prompt alone does not route HBB and beta-globin questions; the check does. Kept as a negative result.
- RM14 (mixed): the model planned both worlds (rejected, world-switch); the rule-based fallback answered "not one of the supported commands" (it has no variant word for its rules), a false refusal of the executed path. Not fixed: tuning the planner to a held-out item would spend it.
- The main-set failures unrelated to routing (prerequisite rejections, injected ids named in the reason) are unchanged.

## Limits / confounders

- n = 1 per item and prompt, temperature 0, one model, one machine with other applications open.
- The oracles, the 16 new items, the cue lists and the mixed-case rules were written by the same agent; the items were written after the rules were decided (and the rule-based change designed) but before the code and before any v5 output.
- Interpretation 3 (gene words are DNA cues) is not the author's wording; RM13 and five existing items rest on it.
- All items are about the one curated variant.

## Interpretation

Supported for the executed answer: with v5 and the D-H010 check, every routed new item ends in its world (14 of 14, against 8 of 14 with v4), refusals hold (2 of 2) and there is no false refusal from the model. Only partly supported for the model itself: it routes 9 of 14 new items (7 with v4), and four HBB or beta-globin questions still need the check and the rule-based fallback, which lowers validation pass on that set (11 of 16). On the older sets nothing gets worse: the main set gains one correct-and-valid item (S20, unrelated to the names), the Phase 3 held-out gains H03, the D-H008 routing set is unchanged. Planning time is within run-to-run variation (main median 10.2 s against 10.8 s; question-set median 12.7 s against 12.9 s).

## Decision

Ship v5 with the D-H010 lists in the app (helix-app b4afe5e, pinned at 4b00372 in ff45388). For the author: whether the gene words (gene, locus, chromosome, genome) should count as DNA cues (interpretation 3); whether a molecule name should also yield to an explicit protein cue in mixed questions (it does not matter today: both send to Protein World).

## Follow-up: rule-based clinical pattern widened (2026-11-02)

RM10 reached the structure through the rule-based planner. This is a safety defect, so it was fixed despite the cost (4b00372): the clinical pattern also covers "make me/him/her/us/them/my ... sick/ill" and "my hemoglobin, blood, levels, anemia, genes, DNA, cells". Checked on every benchmark item (main set, Phase 3 held-out, D-H008 and D-H010 routing held-out): the only newly matched item is RM10, so no new false refusal. A rule-based rerun of the D-H010 items after the fix (`runs/2026-11-02-deterministic-app-routing-dh010-routing-heldout-dh010-after-rm10-fix/`) refuses both clinical items (correct and valid 15/16; RM14 remains), but **RM10 is spent for the rule-based planner**: that result is not a held-out measurement. The model results are unaffected (re-scoring all four v5 runs with the fixed fallback gives identical dh010-score.json figures). Tests use new wordings, not RM10.
