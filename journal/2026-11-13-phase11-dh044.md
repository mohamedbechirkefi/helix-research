## Methods

D-H044 (author, 2026-11-13): (1) the sentence for a gene outside the build ("... only <OPEN> is.") is kept; (2) the rules recognise the build's curated set symbols as gene names. Before the change, the foreign-gene step's filter accepted an upper-case token only if it was in the rules' list of well-known genes (`OUT_OF_CONTEXT_GENES`, which has CFTR and BRCA1) or matched `/^[A-Z]{2,6}\d{1,2}[A-Z]?$/` (which matches FAAH2 and BRCA1, not SLC6A3, whose symbol ends in a digit after a letter, nor HBB, which has no digit). The change adds one condition: the token is among `RuleOptions.buildSets`, upper-cased (helix-research e390be0, 6 lines in `packages/ai-planning/src/fallback.ts`). The list is the one D-H043 added: the Director's `curatedSets` option, which the app's controller fills from the datasets index; without it nothing is newly recognised. The open set's symbols and aliases are still excluded, and the sentence is chosen by the D-H043 `foreignGeneReason`, unchanged.

**Case.** The tokens come from `/\b[A-Z][A-Z0-9]{1,7}\b/g`, which has no case-insensitive flag: the existing rule never recognised a lower-case symbol ("show cftr" was not a foreign gene before D-H044 either). The build's symbols follow the same handling: "show slc6a3" is out of scope and keeps its earlier answer. Both repositories' pins test it. The build list itself is matched whatever its case.

In the app, `APP_RULES_VERSION` goes from 5 to 6 (helix-app 2ad88ef; the three version tests updated) and the research pin moves to e390be0. No other app code changed; `scripts/rules-score.ts` gained only a comment after the first score.

Constant: the curated data, every other rule, the prompts (v8), the trusted context, the claim screen, the budgets.

## Limits / confounders

The frozen sets contain no question that names another gene of the build, so their identical scores show that nothing else changed, not that the new recognition is right; that rests on the unit tests in both repositories. Recognition covers the build's symbols only, in upper case: a lower-case symbol, an alias or a full name of another curated gene (for example "dopamine transporter" on the HBB set) is not recognised as another set, as before. The list is the build's (five sets here); a build with other sets would recognise its own symbols. A symbol that is a common upper-case word would be recognised too; none of the five is one. One machine, Chrome only.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis holds within what was tested: another curated gene typed as its symbol now gets the D-H043 sentence on every set, for 18 bytes at rest, with identical outcomes on both frozen sets as the author expected, and no suite regressed.
