## Measurements

Raw results in helix-app `apps/web/e2e/results/*dh038*`; the review is helix-app `docs/reviews/phase-11-accessibility-audit.md` section 11.

Each item was pinned first (607004a: S21 checks failing in Chrome and Safari, the layout notes check failing on 8 screens, a design-system test), then built:

1. A polite status line once an ask lands: "Answer ready: ", "Clarification needed: " or "Refused: " then the explanation's first sentence, which the rules write (D-H029 answer 88). One text node, exposed in all three browsers' trees.
2. ArrowDown in a focused codon opens its three bases in the tape, each named (for example "A, base 2 of codon 7, GAG"); Left and Right step, Enter or Space picks through the same path as a mouse pick; ArrowUp returns. Escape inside the bases is not handled (left out to save bytes).
3. Frame rule kept at 16.8 ms; budgets.mjs reruns a breached frame file once and records both runs.
4. While a layer is open, the world notes stay in the 64 px band, one line each, cut with an ellipsis when short of space (full text in the title); bottom edge at most 60 px.
5. A world button while Evidence World is open changes the world underneath; Evidence World stays open with "Over Protein World" (or DNA, Pathway) in its header; the switcher marks the world with aria-pressed; focus stays on the button.
6. Selects use `appearance: none` with a drawn arrow; the same focus ring in all three browsers.

## Interpretation

All six items hold under automated checks in the three engines (Safari verified by the agent only). The bytes budget now has no room: any further shipped change needs space made first or a decision on the budget. The announcement line is read from the explanation's first sentence; the composer always writes a sentence for refusals and clarifications, and every answer exercised by the suites has one; an empty answer would read "undefined" (not guarded, to save bytes; not reached by any suite).
