## Method

1. The English master was read against the records of Phases 11 and 12 and brought to draft 0.9:
   - the header, the contents, 1.5 (contributions for every phase);
   - 9.9 (the documentation and what Phase 12 leaves open);
   - chapter 10 (Safari diagnosis, P12-BOR-01 and -02, the host header file, the desktop items not fixed, the usability fixes);
   - the cost (C200), chapter 13 (the install order, C199), Annex A.
2. Stale status lines left from earlier phases were removed: the chapter 9 heading note, three "In progress" notes, the open verdicts of 9.5 and 9.6.
3. An AI agent applied the French equivalents and made a full pass of the French against the English; the coordinating session then made the later edits in both files.
4. A second AI agent drafted the article from the frozen report, checking by script that every claim id it cites is in the register and in the report, and that every number token occurs in one of them. The coordinating session reviewed it, removed one derived figure (a difference of two measured values) and corrected two phrasings ("as the report states them", "an independent AI audit"). The first agent translated it.
5. The bibliography links were checked again.

## Findings

- **The parity script missed three-digit claim ids.** Its pattern matched `C` and two digits only, so C100 to C200 were compared only as numbers. It now matches three digits.
- **The French copy had drifted in one paragraph.** It used decimal commas (22,7 s) where the rest of the French follows the English, and "build" where the copy elsewhere says "construction". Both were corrected.
- **The register had two gaps the article draft found.**
  - C187 spoke of 16 provenance hashes, where C167 and chapter 10 give 14: the 16 differences are the 2 generator fields and the 14 hashes. The row is corrected.
  - The 9,126 displayed texts of 9.9 had no row; C197 now carries it.
- **The privacy incident** of chapter 11 has no claim row, so the article leaves it out.

## Uncertainty

The article's number check is a substring test, weak for small numbers; the figures it rests on were read against their rows. The French texts were written by an AI agent and reviewed by the coordinating session by script and spot reading, not by a native reader other than the author.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis holds: the report is complete as draft 0.9 with a French copy identical in every checked count, and the article in both languages cites only register rows used by the report. None of the three documents is published. The report files follow the repository's practice for working drafts. The article stays on a local branch until the author's word (G-PUBLIC, question 109 (a)).
