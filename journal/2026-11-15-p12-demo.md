## Method

The recorder drives the served release build in Chrome and keeps Chrome's DevTools screencast frames. A second page then draws those frames on a canvas at their recorded times, adding the cards, chapter captions, highlights and speed badges. Chrome's MediaRecorder encodes the canvas.

The script for each group:
- **A:** HBB from the welcome; the reference question; the rules' first suggested follow-up; the wheel zoom from codon to base pair; Evidence World; CFTR (captured, left out of the cut).
- **B:** PTEN through "Retrieve live".
- **C:** "Show it as a surface." for the local model, after the rules' answer.

Frames from the encoded file were drawn at twelve times and inspected.

- The evidence highlight aimed at a list below the fold, so group A was captured again.
- A held frame kept the previous chapter's caption across a chapter boundary.

## Uncertainty

This is one take on one machine. The machine was not quiet, and no timing claim is made from the recording. The timings are measured by the suites. The model segment runs on the development page in Chrome, not in the desktop app. The optional desktop segment was not recorded.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis holds: every P12.2.2 item is in the take, from the real app, with no fallback and no staged shot (C198).
