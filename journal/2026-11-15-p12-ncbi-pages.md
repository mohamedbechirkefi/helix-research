## Method

Both pages are behind reCAPTCHA for scripts, so the author opened them in a browser and pasted their text into the session:
- ClinVar, "Accessing and using data in ClinVar" (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/docs/maintenance_use/), page footer "Last updated: 2024-06-04";
- RefSeq Help, "RefSeq Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)" (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK50679/), "Last Update: May 30, 2025".

The paste of the RefSeq FAQ was cut at the session's message limit, in the section "How are exons annotated on RefSeqs?". The sections after it (curation, updates and removal) were not read.

The statements were compared with the ClinVar attribution string (helix-research `packages/data-adapters/src/sources.ts`, source `ncbi-clinvar`) and the nonclinical notices (helix-app `apps/web/src/phase6.tsx`, both READMEs).

## Raw results

| Page | Statement | Helix |
|---|---|---|
| ClinVar | "The information on this website is not intended for direct diagnostic use or medical decision-making without review by a genetics professional." | The app says it is nonclinical, "not a diagnostic, variant classification or medical advice tool" (Phase 6 screen; both READMEs) |
| ClinVar | "If you distribute or copy data from ClinVar, we ask that you provide attribution to ClinVar as a data source in publications and websites. You can cite one of the ClinVar publications (e.g. PMID: 29165669)." | The on-screen attribution names "NCBI ClinVar via E-utilities" as the source (C158). No ClinVar publication is cited, on screen or in the report |
| ClinVar | No restriction on use or redistribution | none needed |
| RefSeq FAQ (part read) | Citation guidance only: cite the accession with its version, the NCBI Handbook, or the RefSeq article (O'Leary et al., NAR 2016). No usage restriction | RefSeq data are not shipped as a separate source; RefSeq accessions inside Ensembl and ClinVar records are shown with their version |

## Findings

- **The ClinVar attribution request is met.** ClinVar is named as the data source on screen. Citing a ClinVar publication is offered ("You can cite"), not required. It is left out, like the PDB primary citations and the Open Targets paper (P11-LIC-09, a note).
- **The diagnostic disclaimer matches what Helix already says.**
- **No RefSeq usage restriction was found** in the part of the FAQ that was read.
- P11-LIC-07 and audit section 5 item 4 are closed.

## Uncertainty

The text was read from the author's paste, not fetched by a script. The RefSeq FAQ was read only as far as the paste reached.
