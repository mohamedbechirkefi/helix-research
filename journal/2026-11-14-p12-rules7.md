## Methods

**Fields.** Per curated set, from its gene record (`data/curated/<set>/gene.json`) as helix-app `apps/web/scripts/copy-data.mjs` writes the datasets index: `symbol` (HGNC approved symbol), `aliases` (HGNC alias symbols) and `name` (Ensembl's `description` without its "[Source:...]" tail, the HGNC approved name). The records carry no previous symbols (the build's HGNC records have `previousSymbols: []`) and no protein name, so neither is used. No hand-written list.

**Rule** (helix-research bf592c1, `buildGeneMentions` in `packages/ai-planning/src/fallback.ts`; new `RuleOptions.buildGenes`): for every curated set other than the open one, its symbol, aliases and name are terms; the question and each term are folded (accents removed, lower case); a term matches whole-word (no letter or digit immediately before or after), with any run of whitespace inside a name; a term must have at least three characters and a letter; the open set's symbol, aliases and name are blanked out of the question first, and its gene is skipped; a term shared by two sets is not used (none is). The symbols of `buildSets` are matched in any case too (an older caller passing only symbols gets lower-case symbols). A capitals token the earlier rule read as a gene outside the build (for example the alias BRCC1, which the pattern `/^[A-Z]{2,6}\d{1,2}[A-Z]?$/` matches) is replaced by the curated set it lies inside. Genes are named in the order of the question, and the sentence is chosen by the D-H043 `foreignGeneReason`, unchanged; the set is named by its symbol. A gene outside the build is still recognised in capitals only ("show tp53" is unchanged).

**Accepted terms** (all 25; none excluded by the rule): BRCA1: BRCA1, BRCC1, FANCS, PPP1R53, RNF53, "BRCA1 DNA repair associated". CFTR: CFTR, ABC35, CFTR/MRP, MRP7, TNR-CFTR, dJ760C5.1, "CF transmembrane conductance regulator". FAAH2: FAAH2, FAAH-2, FLJ31204, RP11-479E16.1, "fatty acid amide hydrolase 2". HBB: HBB, CD113t-C, beta-globin, "hemoglobin subunit beta". SLC6A3: SLC6A3, DAT, "solute carrier family 6 member 3". The one short all-letter alias is DAT, matched as "dat" too; it is not an English word. Not available, so not recognised: DAT1 (previous symbol of SLC6A3), ABCC7, CF (previous symbols of CFTR), "dopamine transporter" (UniProt's protein name).

**App** (helix-app e69bbec): the Director takes a `curatedGenes` option (the datasets index's entries) and passes it to the rules as `buildGenes`; the controller passes `curatedGenes: datasets`; `APP_RULES_VERSION` 6 to 7 (four version tests); research pin e390be0 to bf592c1; `scripts/rules-score.ts` passes each set's gene symbol, name and aliases from its context, as the controller does from the index. No other app code changed.

## Limits / confounders

The frozen sets contain no question naming another set's term, so their identical scores show that nothing else changed, not that the new recognition is right; that rests on the unit tests and the probe. The examples "DAT1" and "dopamine transporter" are not recognised as SLC6A3: the curated gene records carry neither previous symbols nor protein names, and the rule reads only the records. A set is named by its symbol when the visitor typed an alias or a name ("Show DAT" gets "SLC6A3 is one of this build's curated sets ..."). A name is recognised only whole ("solute carrier" alone is not). "dat" in lower case is recognised as SLC6A3. On CFTR "Show the hemoglobin" still enters Protein World on CFTR (protein-world-named, unchanged and outside this change). Character positions of the folded question are used only to order the genes named. One machine, Chrome only.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis holds within what was tested: another curated gene typed in lower case, as an HGNC alias or as its HGNC name now gets the D-H043 sentence, for 406 bytes at rest, with identical outcomes on both frozen sets and both replays passing with the allowlist unchanged.

## Decision

**Continue.** For the author: whether previous symbols (and protein names) should enter the curated records so that "DAT1" and "dopamine transporter" are recognised (a data change, not a rules change), and whether the sentence should echo the typed alias.
